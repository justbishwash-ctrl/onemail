Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026 · Last Updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

OneMail ("OneMail", "we", "us", or "our") is a free, open-source email client available at onemail.gdn.com.np .

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use OneMail.

2. Information We Collect

OneMail collects limited account information through Google OAuth when you sign in or connect your Google account.

The information we retain is:

  • Name — used to identify your OneMail account.
  • Email address — used to identify your OneMail account and associate it with your connected Google account.

We do not require you to provide additional personal information to use OneMail.

3. Gmail Data and Google API Access

OneMail uses Google OAuth and the Gmail API to provide email-client functionality.

Depending on the permissions you authorize, OneMail may access Gmail data necessary to:

  • Read and display email messages.
  • Compose and send emails.
  • Move emails to the Trash when you request that action.
OneMail does not permanently store your Gmail messages or attachments on its own application servers.

Your Gmail data remains in your Google account. Gmail data may be temporarily processed in transit or memory when necessary to display a message or perform an action requested by you.

4. How We Use Information

We use your name and email address to identify and maintain your OneMail account and connect your Google account.

Gmail data is used only to provide the email features requested by you, including reading, sending, and moving emails to the Trash.

We do not sell your personal information or Gmail data. We do not use Gmail data for advertising or advertising profiling.

We do not use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

OneMail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

  • We request only permissions necessary for OneMail's email functionality.
  • We use Google user data only to provide user-facing features that are visible and requested by users.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties for unrelated purposes.

6. Storage and Security

Your name and email address may be stored as necessary to maintain your OneMail account.

We do not permanently store Gmail message content, mailbox contents, or attachments on our application servers.

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the information handled by OneMail. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information or Gmail data for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

OneMail may use Google services and infrastructure necessary to authenticate your account and provide the functionality you request.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or legal process.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your name and email address only for as long as necessary to maintain your OneMail account, provide the service, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may disconnect OneMail from your Google account at any time.

You may request deletion of retained OneMail account information by contacting:

bishwashn325@gmail.com

Disconnecting OneMail does not delete emails from your Gmail account.

9. Revoking Google Access

You can revoke OneMail's access to your Google account through your Google Account security settings.

Once access is revoked, OneMail will no longer be able to use the revoked permissions to access your Gmail account.

11. Children's Privacy

OneMail does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
